The process before funding.

Requesting access is not a commitment. Either party may decline to proceed at any stage.

  • 01

    You send this form

    Both principals review each submission and respond within one business day.

  • 02

    We speak for thirty minutes

    We review your objectives, timeline and tax position, and answer your questions. If we do not consider this an appropriate fit, we will say so during the call.

  • 03

    You verify and review

    Third-party accreditation verification, followed by the complete offering package: memorandum, underwriting model, third-party reports and operating agreement.

Correspondence is handled directly by the principals. There is no automated follow-up sequence. If an offering is oversubscribed we will advise you and add you to the list for the next one.
