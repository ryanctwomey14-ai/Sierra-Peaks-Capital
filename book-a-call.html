Schedule an introductory call.

The initial conversation is an assessment of fit. We will ask about your objectives and answer your questions, and we will tell you during the call if we do not believe this is appropriate for your situation.

What the call covers.

  • 01

    Your objectives

    Your timeline, tax position and investment objectives. We are not able to provide personalised advice, but we can indicate whether this asset class is consistent with what you have described.

  • 02

    How we operate

    Acquisition criteria, debt structure, fee arrangements and principal co-investment. Questions are welcome at any point.

  • 03

    An assessment of fit

    If passive multifamily, the timing, or this particular sponsor is not appropriate for your circumstances, we will say so directly.

  • 04

    Documentation

    If both parties wish to proceed, we begin third-party accreditation verification and provide the complete offering file. No documents are executed during the call.

Prefer to proceed in writing? You may complete the qualification form instead. We respond within one business day.

Determining fit.

The following should help you determine whether a conversation is worthwhile before scheduling one.

This is likely a fit if

You are an accredited investor, the capital is not required for the duration of the hold period, quarterly income and depreciation are more valuable to you than daily liquidity, and you intend to review the offering documents in full.

This is likely not a fit if

You are not yet accredited, the capital may be required before the end of the hold period, you want involvement in operating decisions, or you are evaluating sponsors primarily on headline return figures.
