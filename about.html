Sierra Peaks / About

Built on strong relationships.

Sierra Peaks Capital was built from a desire to create something meaningful — both financially and personally. The mission is simple: build wealth through disciplined real estate investing while creating opportunities that benefit both our investors and the communities we serve.

Our story

We believe strong investments are built on strong relationships. That means operating with trust, clear communication, and long-term alignment with the people we partner with.

The foundation of the company is rooted in growth, resilience, and a commitment to doing things the right way. Every step forward is driven by a focus on improving, learning, and executing at a higher level.

This isn’t about chasing deals or short-term wins. It’s about building a long-term platform grounded in strong fundamentals, meaningful relationships, and consistent execution.

The principals.

Sierra Peaks is a two-principal firm. Both principals underwrite every acquisition and handle investor communication directly.

Dale Harding

Co-Founder

Dale Harding is a co-founder of Sierra Peaks Capital, focused on identifying and executing multifamily investment opportunities with a practical, results-driven approach.

He brings a strong emphasis on understanding the fundamentals of each deal — looking at how a property operates today, where improvements can be made, and how to create real, measurable value over time.

Dale’s perspective is grounded in consistency and follow-through. He focuses on making disciplined decisions, managing details, and ensuring that each investment performs as expected throughout the hold period.

His role is centered on execution — helping turn strategy into results while maintaining a clear focus on performance, efficiency, and long-term outcomes for investors.

Austin Lamberth

Co-Founder

Austin Lamberth is a co-founder of Sierra Peaks Capital, focused on acquiring and operating multifamily real estate through a disciplined, value-driven approach.

His path into real estate has been shaped by a deep commitment to growth, responsibility, and building something meaningful — not just for himself, but for his family and the people he works with.

He believes real leadership is built on accountability, consistency, and doing what you say you are going to do. That mindset carries into every investment, where the focus is on clear execution, strong decision-making, and delivering results the right way.

Austin approaches this business with a long-term vision — building not just a portfolio, but a platform rooted in trust, strong relationships, and performance. His goal is to create opportunities that generate real impact while holding a high standard in how those opportunities are executed.

What you actually receive.

Reporting and communication provided to limited partners throughout the hold period.

  • Q

    Quarterly distribution by ACH

    Paid within 30 days of quarter end, directly to the account you nominate. No paper cheques, no chasing.

  • Q

    Quarterly asset report

    Occupancy, trailing collections, renovation progress against plan, and budget variance with a written explanation of anything that changed. Six pages, not sixty.

  • A

    Annual audited financial statements

    Prepared by an independent CPA firm and delivered with the annual investor letter.

  • A

    Annual Schedule K-1

    Prepared and issued each year for your tax filing. We prioritise timely delivery, as late K-1s are a common source of frustration in private real estate.

  • ∞

    Direct line to a principal

    Questions regarding your position are handled directly by one of the two principals.
