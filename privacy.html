Privacy policy

Sierra Peaks Capital, LLC respects the confidentiality of the information you provide. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Name, email address, telephone number, accreditation basis, indicative investment size, timeline, investing vehicle and anything you write in a message field.
  • Information collected automatically. IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and pages viewed, through standard web analytics.
  • Information from verification partners. If you proceed toward an investment, our third-party accreditation verification service returns a pass or fail result. We do not receive or store your underlying tax returns, asset statements or credit reports.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a conversation.
  • To determine whether you are eligible to receive offering materials under Rule 506(c).
  • To administer an investment, including distributions, reporting and tax documents.
  • To send occasional educational material, which you may stop at any time.
  • To comply with securities, anti-money-laundering and tax obligations.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

Only service providers who need it to do their work for us — our investor relationship management system, email provider, accreditation verification service, fund administrator, accountants and legal counsel — each bound by confidentiality obligations. We also disclose information where required by law, subpoena or regulatory request.

Retention and security

Investor records are retained for the period required by securities and tax law, generally at least seven years after an investment ends. Enquiry records from people who do not invest are retained for up to three years. We use encryption in transit, access controls and multi-factor authentication, though no method of transmission or storage is entirely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do neither), and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. Email privacy@sierrapeakscapital.com and we will respond within the period the applicable law requires. Note that records we must keep under securities or tax law cannot be deleted on request.

Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies for basic site function and aggregate analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work. We honour Global Privacy Control signals where required.

Email

Every marketing email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Transactional messages relating to an existing investment — distribution notices, tax documents, quarterly reports — will continue as long as you are an investor, because we are obliged to send them.
